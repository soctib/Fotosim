Camera panel – top-view drafts

Every camera body and lens type in Fotosim's data, seen from above with the lens pointing away from the photographer. Bodies and lenses share one scale (mm), so sizes compare honestly. Dashed red line on a lens front = entrance pupil (focal length ÷ largest aperture) at the shown focal length.

3 · Combinations

Realistic body + lens pairings with sample settings on the top display and rings. Zooms are shown at both ends to show the barrel extension.

4 · Try it

Operate the camera itself: drag the zoom and focus rings sideways, tap or drag the mode dial, drag the front dial (under the shutter button) and the rear dial (back edge), press ISO, or drag a value on the display. The sliders on the side stay in sync – the “traditional UI” half of the idea. A simple light meter (scene at EV 13) lets the camera pick the values it controls in A, P, Av and Tv; in M the display shows the meter bar.
