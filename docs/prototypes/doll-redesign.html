Our doll, Personas head

The Personas outline for head, neck and ears and its hair shapes and colours, with our own eyes, nose and mouth kept. Three body drafts sit next to the current one. Every blurred frame uses the app's depth split: ears 9 cm behind the eyes, face and body in the eye plane, nose tip 3 cm in front.

What changed

  • Head: the Personas shape (a rounded capsule with ear bumps and a neck in one outline). The face gets a lighter skin tone and the neck and ears the base tone, like the original. The ears get a small inner fold so focusing on them has something to resolve.
  • Hair: Personas shapes; person 2 gets a different style (pick one in the hair gallery below). The highlight colours are pre-mixed, because the original blend mode does nothing inside a blurred group.
  • Kept: our detailed eyes (iris, catchlight, lid) and mouth.
  • Nose (new): our triangle with a flat shadow on its left half. The whole nose fades out from the tip up to the bridge, so it blends into the face. The nostrils, freckles and highlight stay for the close-ups. The flat nose is still shown next to it for comparison.
  • Bodies: A keeps the current body. B has rounded shoulders, a neckline, sleeves with hands, trousers with two legs, and shoes. C is B with short sleeves, bare forearms, jeans with cuffs and sneakers, and a narrower torso (shoulders ±19.6 cm instead of ±22.8 cm).
  • The Personas outlines are CC BY 4.0 (Draftbit), so we need a credit line ("based on Personas by Draftbit, modified").
